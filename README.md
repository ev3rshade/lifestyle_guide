## Overcoming Obstacles

### A | Brain fog / Fatigue
Ask myself: what could be causing this brain fog?
- Lack of food --> eat
- Dehydration --> drink water
- Lack of sleep --> write everything down step by step, dumb it down so my brain understands

### B  | Distraction
Ask myself: what is causing my brain to drift?
- Focused for a long time --> Take a break
- Physical distraction --> change environment by removing the distraction
- Emotional distress --> stop doing work, take 10 minutes to write about what's distracting me
- Fatigue --> take a break, look at A

## C | Lack of Motivation
Ask myeslf: why am I losing drive?
- Lonliness --> Go talk to someone. Phone a friend.
- Loss of confidence in self --> Take a shower

### D | Task Paralysis
Ask myself: why am I putting off this task?
- Fear --> Go talk to someone about it. Schedule time to do the task.
- Tediousness --> Can I delegate? Schedule time to do the task.

### E | Unable to Communicate Well
First, deep breath. 
Ask myself: what is the goal of this communication?
- Knowledge --> Come up with questions related to what I wanted to learn about the topic.
- Human connection --> Explain that I want to get closer to the person. Remind myself that I need to talk about myself as well for it to be a conversation.

If possible, before a conversation, come up with a conversation outline.
